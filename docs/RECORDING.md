# Recording the demo

Notes for whoever is holding the microphone. Four clips, about ten minutes of
work, no editing required beyond trimming the ends.

Cairn appears in screen recordings — it is excluded from capture only for the
instant its own screenshot is taken. So OBS, Xbox Game Bar (`Win+G`) and Teams
all record it normally.

## Before you start

- Close anything you would not want a manager to see. Cairn dims the screen but
  records whatever is behind it.
- Use **one monitor** if you can. Cairn covers the screen the question was asked
  on, which is correct behaviour but reads as confusing in a recording that
  shows both.
- Check the panel says `Ctrl Space` in its hint. If an IME has taken that
  chord, Cairn falls through to another and the hint tells you which — say the
  right one out loud.
- Have a real, unfamiliar settings page open. A page you clearly know well
  undercuts the point.

---

## Clip 1 — the thing itself (45 seconds)

The one that has to work. Everything else is supporting material.

1. Start in an application, not on the desktop. Something with a settings panel.
2. Press `Ctrl+Space`.
3. Click **Speak** and ask a real question — *"where do I change the billing
   address"*.
4. Say nothing while it answers. Let the pointing and the narration play.
5. Press `Esc`.

Say over the top of it: it read the screen, it is pointing at the real
application, and nothing was pasted into a chat window.

## Clip 2 — the second time is free (30 seconds)

The commercial argument, and it only lands if clip 1 came first.

1. After clip 1's answer, click **Save as trail**.
2. Press `Esc`, then `Ctrl+Space` again.
3. Ask the same question in **different words**.
4. Point out the chip: it says the answer was recalled, and the time next to it
   is milliseconds rather than seconds.

The line worth saying: the first person pays for the answer, everybody after
them gets it for nothing.

## Clip 3 — it works anywhere (20 seconds)

1. Switch to something that is not a browser — Explorer, Excel, a PDF viewer,
   an installer dialog.
2. `Ctrl+Space`, ask something about it, let it point.

This is the clip that answers "why not just a Chrome extension", so make sure
the application on screen is obviously not a browser.

## Clip 4 — what it does not do (20 seconds)

Optional, and worth more than it looks. Managers trust a demo that admits its
edges.

1. Open the tray menu. Show **Start with Windows** unchecked and
   **Check connection**.
2. Say: no keys in the download, the screenshot is taken when you ask and not
   stored, and it is one portable file you delete to uninstall.

## If something goes wrong on camera

- **Nothing happens on the hotkey** — an IME has it. Open the tray; the tooltip
  names the chord Cairn actually got.
- **"Couldn't reach Cairn's server"** — tray → **Check connection**. It names
  the cause rather than shrugging.
- **The marker is beside the target rather than on it** — the coordinates came
  back loose. Re-ask with a more specific question; do not narrate it as a bug
  unless it happens repeatedly.
